Standing goals

Hours per day by weekday. Individual days can still be overridden on the Today tab.
A goal of 0 marks a rest day, which never breaks a streak.

Private mode

Off for everyone by default. These are two separate switches because they are two separate wishes — you can take either one without the other.
Everything is visible both ways.

Study reminders

A nudge on this device at a time you pick, on days you have not logged anything. Nothing is emailed, and nobody else can see whether you have them on.
Reminders are off.
Calendar reminder
Works even where notifications are switched off, because your calendar delivers it rather than the browser. Add this link once and you get a daily prompt plus every exam you have dated.
In Google Calendar, go to Other calendars → + → From URL, paste, then Add. Treat the link like a password: anyone who has it can read your exam dates.

Subjects and areas

Subjects are your courses. Areas are what you actually sit down and revise inside them. Target hours and current mark are optional but make your stats far more useful.
Each subject arrives with its 2026 HSC exam dates and its course sections already filled in. Rename, delete or add to any of it afterwards.
No exam date or course sections come with a subject typed in by hand — you can fill both in yourself below.

Import and export

Bring in a backup from the solo tracker, or a CSV

Accepts the JSON backup exported by the solo study tracker, or a CSV with the columns date, subject, area, from, to, minutes, mode, note. From and To are required, as 9:05 or 9:05 pm. Minutes is worked out from them, so it can be left blank. Missing subjects and areas are created for you.

About and privacy

Everyone signed in can see everyone else's sessions, hours and notes. That is the whole point of the app — but it does mean write your notes as if your mates are reading them, because they are.

Your subjects, areas and goals are yours. You can edit or delete your own sessions whenever you like; nobody else can, and that is enforced by the database rather than this page. An administrator can remove entries that break the rules, and every removal they make is logged.

Your streak counts consecutive days where you met your goal. A day with a goal of zero is a rest day and passes through without breaking it.

Danger zone.
Clearing your sessions wipes every block you have logged. Your subjects, areas and goals stay put.
Deleting your account removes everything — sessions, subjects, areas, goals, your profile and your login. You disappear from the peloton leaderboard. There is no undo.
Visible to you because the database recognises this account as an administrator.
